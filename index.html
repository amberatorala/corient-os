Onboarding · Retainers, campaigns, projects

From signed brief to first deliverables in four weeks.

The same flow applies to retainer launches, new campaigns, and one-off projects, scaled to scope. Owned by the Account Manager and Ops. Every step has a clear owner, a clear client input, and a clear output, all noted in WhatsApp with due dates. No formal kickoff. Slip on any input window and the start date moves with the client.

Our unified system

Every lane feeds one funnel.

Lanes are not channels operating in isolation. Brand sets the surface. Top-of-funnel lanes attract attention. Mid-funnel lanes engage and educate. Conversion lanes close the gap to revenue. Retention lanes compound. This is how every active lane maps to the customer journey.

Phase gates

Advance only when these are true.

Reviewed in the monthly marketing call. Signed off by Account Manager and Strategist before the client moves phases. Missing a gate keeps the client in the current phase. Phases apply per active lane, not across the whole retainer.

  • Positioning and brand approved
  • Website live and converting at baseline
  • Twelve SEO articles live (cornerstone + supporting)
  • Organic Social cadence held weekly
  • Paid Ads CPL and ROAS baselines captured
  • Welcome series live, list capture working
  • Notion Dashboard and Brief established. Agency Analytics dashboard live.

  • Meta and Google at target ROAS for 30 plus days
